# NURS 151 · History and Development of Nursing Self-Test

**Open the test: https://dokuemmanuelantwi-gif.github.io/nurs151-self-test-1/**

A self-assessment test drawn from a bank of 240 questions, with a briefing video, three levels and two modes:

- **Levels:** Beginner (30 questions, 15 minutes), Intermediate (60, 30 minutes) or Advanced (120, 60 minutes)
- **Modes:** Exam (timed, answers at the end) or Practice (untimed, answers after each question)
- **Question types:** multiple choice, fill in the blanks, matching pairs, diagrams and theory

Your name, answers and scores stay in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

`index.html` is the whole app in one file. The copy shared directly with students is the same file, named `NURS151-History-and-Development-of-Nursing-Self-Test.html`.
